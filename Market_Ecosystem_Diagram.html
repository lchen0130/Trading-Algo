Order Flow

Retail orders travel: Investor → Broker → Wholesaler (~90% internalized off-exchange) or → Exchange (~10%). Institutional orders go directly to exchanges or dark pools via execution algorithms (VWAP, TWAP, SOR). Market makers and HFT firms post limit orders to exchanges, earning maker rebates.

Money & Fee Flow

Maker-Taker Model: Exchanges pay ~$0.002/share rebates to limit order posters (makers) and charge ~$0.003/share to market order senders (takers). PFOF: Wholesalers pay brokers for retail order flow (~$0.002-0.004/share). Retail investors receive price improvement (~$0.01/share) but their orders never reach public exchanges.

Data & Regulation

The SIP (Securities Information Processor) consolidates quotes from all exchanges into the NBBO. Data vendors (Bloomberg, Refinitiv) distribute market data, while alternative data providers sell satellite imagery, credit card data, and NLP signals. SEC, FINRA, and CFTC oversee all participants with rules on best execution, risk controls, and audit trails.
